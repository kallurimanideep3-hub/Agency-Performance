Design decisions

  • One tall visual, not several small ones: the job on this page is scanning 186 agents for who is red. Giving Deneb the full width and about 75% of the height keeps every row readable.
  • Slicers directly above the grid: Year and Month define the target period; Region and Territory narrow the list so it fits without scrolling. Month is single-select because a full-year view turns almost every agent green.
  • Sorted by achievement, worst at the bottom: the eye lands on the leaders first; the red tail at the bottom is what a territory manager acts on.
  • Colour carries the status, position carries the size: green, amber and red come from the Target Status measure, so thresholds live in one place in the model.
  • Click vs right-click: a left click cross-filters (dims the others); right-click, Drill through opens that agent on Page 3, because cross-filtering cannot cross pages.
